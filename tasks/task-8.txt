Title: Make the version footer easier to read

As a user reporting a problem, I want the footer to label the version clearly, so that I know what
to copy into a bug report.

Description:
The footer currently reads "v0.0.0 (abc1234)": the version and the short commit SHA in parentheses.
Change it to "Version 0.0.0 · abc1234": the word "Version", the version number without the "v"
prefix, a middle dot, and the short commit SHA.

Acceptance criteria:
- The footer reads "Version <version> · <short sha>", e.g. "Version 0.0.0 · abc1234".
- The version and the SHA still come from the build (package.json and git), as today.
- No other text on the page changes.
- `pnpm build` and `pnpm lint` succeed.

Out of scope:
- Styling changes.
- Showing a build date or anything else in the footer.
