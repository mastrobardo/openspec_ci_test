Title: Show the current year in the footer

As a visitor, I want the footer to show the current year, so that the page does not look abandoned.

Description:
Append the current year to the footer, after what it shows today, separated by " · ". For example
"v0.0.0 (abc1234) · 2026". The year is computed in the browser when the page renders.

Acceptance criteria:
- The footer ends with " · <current year>", e.g. " · 2026".
- The rest of the footer text is unchanged.
- `pnpm build` and `pnpm lint` succeed.

Out of scope:
- A copyright notice or company name.
- Styling changes.
